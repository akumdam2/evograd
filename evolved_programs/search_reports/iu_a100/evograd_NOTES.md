Only the three superseded/ reruns ran with it. Otherwise never edited during the trial. The serial smoke (ADBench/smoke/evograd) ran the unpatched tree at 3930220 with --frontend a --fallback-unified; the patched tree's smoke is ADBench/smoke/evograd_par.
- Task packs: ADBench/tasks, built at 8cff3b9; not rebuilt during the trial.
- Per case: `python candidates/bench.py run --model M --agent evograd --case-id C --budget 10 --time-limit 6000 -- --frontend unified --fallback-a`
- LLM: gpt-5.5 (ADBENCH_MODEL), budget 10 model requests per case. The seed is unified (no LLM request); a case with no accepted unified seed falls back to frontend A in the same run, and A's requests count toward the 10 (run.json seed_frontend / seed_fallback_reason).
- GPU: an Indiana University A100 40GB instance, no allocation limit (user, 2026-10-29: DeltaAI ghx4-interactive slots could not be obtained). Three searches at a time ($T/session_par.sh N=3): their LLM requests overlap, their GPU evaluations (seed verification, Gate 1 and T1 timing) take the GPU lock one at a time (log: $T/state/gpu.lock.log). Not under the lock: unified's sub-second capture at the start of a case, and each driver's idle CUDA context (about 1.4 GB). The other agents searched on DeltaAI GH200s: this agent's T1 timing feedback and Gate 1 ran on an A100 (sm_80). Task packs, corpora and weights were copied byte for byte to the same absolute paths. Scripts: $T/{env_iu_par,session_par}.sh with T=/work/hdd/bcjw/wzhan/adbench_trial; queue = evograd's 58 cases in cases.json order.
- Interrupted runs go to <model>/aborted/ and are rerun once; failures are not rerun.
- Smoke: ADBench/smoke/evograd/qwen3_0_6b; not part of the trial.
